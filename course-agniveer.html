About the Course

Agniveer recruitment (the Agnipath scheme) for the Indian Army, Navy, and Air Force combines a written/computer-based test, a physical fitness test, and a medical exam.

Our comprehensive batch covers the entire written syllabus including General Knowledge, General Science, Mathematics, and Reasoning. We also provide vital physical-fitness guidance to ensure you are ready for all phases of the selection process.

Key Features

  • Online & Offline Coaching availability
  • Thorough Written/CBT exam preparation
  • Physical fitness guidance and tips
  • Updated per Army/Navy/Air Force notifications
