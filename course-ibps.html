About the Course

IBPS runs the Common Recruitment Process each year to select Probationary Officers and Clerks for public sector banks across India.

Our combined PO and Clerk batch is designed to cover both Prelims and Mains thoroughly. The curriculum emphasizes Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning, English, and General/Banking Awareness, preparing you for all sections of these highly competitive exams.

Key Features

  • Combined PO & Clerk batch for holistic preparation
  • Complete Prelims + Mains coverage
  • Dedicated Banking & financial awareness sessions
  • Regular mock tests mimicking the exact exam pattern
